How to Play

Guess a character from the hit MMORPG Final Fantasy XIV! Updates daily.

Characters that can be guessed are from the MSQ as well as major storylines like the raid stories and Hildibrand.

Quest Mode

In Quest Mode, type in the name of a character and their properties will be revealed. The color in the table will tell you how close your guess was from the daily character.

Green indicates an exact match.

Yellow indicates a partial match.

Red indicates no match at all.

⬆⬇ The arrows will indicate whether the answer's property is above/below your guess.

Properties

A description of what each property means in relation to the character follows:

Gender: Character's gender can be Male, Female, or Ambiguous/Non-Binary

Age: How old a character actually is.

Affiliation: What organization(s) the character associates themselves with. Example: Scions of the Seventh Dawn, Ascians, Maelstrom, etc.

Job: What in-game job the character plays as. Jobs in the same role but not an exact match will be yellow (DPS separated!).

Example: Dancer guessed but the character's job is a Machinist, so the job column will be yellow, same with a Summoner and a Pictomancer.

As well as that, Jobs played by NPCs that are called by a different name will be categorized under "special".

Race: What race the character is.

Version Introduction: XIV patch the character was introduced on screen and not just mentioned.
